SUBMISSION STEPS
----------------
1. Go to https://partner.microsoft.com/dashboard
2. Sign in with Microsoft Partner account
3. Click "New submission" → "Apps & games"
4. Enter Package Identity Name: PlayTreeCorporation.PlayTree
5. Enter Publisher: CN=PlayTree Corporation
6. Upload Package.appxmanifest + Assets
7. Upload StoreAssets to Store listing section:
   - Hero image → Hero
   - Screenshots → Screenshots (drag all 6)
   - Trailer → Videos
   - Logo → Logo
8. Paste descriptions from this file
9. Select categories, pricing (Free), ratings
10. Submit for certification (usually 1-3 days)
11. Once approved → appears in Microsoft Store on your PC!

NOTE: Requires a Microsoft Partner Center account ($19 one-time fee for individual dev).
      The package identity must match your Partner Center account publisher ID.
